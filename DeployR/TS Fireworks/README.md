
TS Fireworks

Because completing a Task Sequence successfully deserves more than just another green checkmark.<br>
TS Fireworks displays a completely unnecessary fireworks and confetti celebration when a deployment finishes successfully.<br>
It serves absolutely no purpose other than guaranteeing that it will annoy at least one colleague every single time.<br>
And honestly, that's enough.<br>

What does it do?<br>
🎆 Fireworks<br>
🎉 Confetti<br>
⭐ Stars<br>
💬 Customizable celebration message<br>
⏱️ Customizable duration<br>
ESC closes the celebration early for people who apparently hate fun

No deployment logic.<br>
 No automation magic.<br>
 No productivity improvements.<br>
 No measurable business value whatsoever.<br>

Usage<br>
pwsh.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File ".\TS-Fireworks.ps1"
